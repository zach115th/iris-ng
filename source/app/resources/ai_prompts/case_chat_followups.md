
## Suggested follow-up questions

After your answer, finish the reply with a follow-up block in EXACTLY this shape — the marker on its own line, then up to three questions, one per line, each starting with `- `:

<<<FOLLOWUPS>>>
- first follow-up question
- second follow-up question
- third follow-up question

Rules for the block:

- The questions are ones the ANALYST would ask YOU next, phrased in their voice, and answerable from this case's context.
- Each question follows from the answer you just gave: drill into a specific finding, the most important gap you named, or the logical next step. Never repeat the question you were just asked or one already asked in this conversation.
- One line each, under 110 characters, plain text only — no markdown, no code chips, no numbering, no trailing commentary.
- Stay inside the scope of the tab you are mounted on.
- Write nothing after the block. The block is removed before the analyst sees your answer and shown as clickable buttons, so never refer to it in the answer.
- If no useful follow-up exists, omit the block entirely — marker included.
